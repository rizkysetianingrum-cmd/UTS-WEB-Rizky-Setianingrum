About Mivatcha

Welcome to Mivatcha! We are a matcha-inspired store dedicated to bringing delightful matcha drinks and desserts to your everyday moments. With a variety of flavors and creations, Mivatcha offers something special for every matcha lover.

We believe that enjoying matcha is more than just having a drink. It is about creating little moments of happiness, comfort, and relaxation in the middle of your day.

Our Story

Mivatcha was created from a love for matcha and the desire to introduce different ways to enjoy its unique flavor. Inspired by the growing interest in matcha, we developed a collection of drinks and desserts with various flavors and combinations.

From classic matcha drinks to creative desserts, every menu is designed to bring a fresh and enjoyable experience. Mivatcha aims to become a cozy place where everyone can enjoy their own little matcha moment.

Our Vision

To become a creative and welcoming matcha brand that brings enjoyable matcha experiences to everyone.

Our Mission

  • To provide a variety of quality matcha drinks and desserts.
  • To create unique and enjoyable matcha flavor combinations.
  • To introduce the enjoyment of matcha through creative products.

Our Goals

Mivatcha aims to continuously develop creative matcha products, provide enjoyable experiences, and build a community of people who appreciate matcha drinks and desserts.
